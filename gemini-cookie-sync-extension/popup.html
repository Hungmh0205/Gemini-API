Gemini Cookie Sync

Pushes cookies, XSRF, and build metadata from the current Gemini session straight to your local server, which saves gemini-auth.json and re-authenticates automatically. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Ready.

Open Gemini, sign in, then refresh the page before sync. The extension also auto-syncs in the background every few minutes while Chrome is running (a hidden tab is opened if needed). The session data is sensitive; do not share it or commit it to Git.
